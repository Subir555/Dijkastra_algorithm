About Our Project

Welcome to our interactive Dijkstra's Algorithm Visualizer! This web-based tool is built to demonstrate how shortest-path graph algorithms operate in real-time, featuring dynamic SVG animations, custom graph controls, and pre-loaded sample datasets.
